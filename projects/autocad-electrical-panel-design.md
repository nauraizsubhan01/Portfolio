## 📂 Planned Contents

| Section | Description |
|---|---|
| **Power Schematics** | Incoming supply, protection, and power distribution circuits |
| **Control Schematics** | Control logic, interlocks, relays, contactors and I/O circuits |
| **Panel Layouts** | Enclosure arrangement, DIN-rail and duct placement, component spacing |
| **Wiring & Terminals** | Wiring diagrams, terminal strip plans and wire numbering |
| **Reports** | Bills of materials, component lists and from/to wire reports |

## 🛠️ Skills Demonstrated

- Project-based drawing management in AutoCAD Electrical
- Schematic and panel symbol libraries, with cross-referencing between drawings
- Industrial drafting standards (IEC / NFPA conventions)
- Producing documentation that a panel builder can work from directly
